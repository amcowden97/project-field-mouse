# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Project Field Mouse is committed to a welcoming, respectful community for hobbyists,
educators, researchers, conservation groups, operators, and contributors of every
background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Welcome questions and explain decisions without belittling others.
- Focus criticism on ideas and observable behavior.
- Respect privacy, field-site sensitivity, wildlife welfare, and research ethics.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate
disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, reviews, events, and public
spaces where someone represents the project. Report conduct concerns privately through
the repository's security-reporting path described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or ban
participants when needed. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, and
retaliation against reporters is prohibited.

This policy is intentionally concise and may be replaced by a formally adopted
community code as governance grows.
